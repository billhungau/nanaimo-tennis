# Content method

Indoor Tennis Nanaimo is a community information project. The site separates documented facts, announced intentions, community proposals, candidate statements, and unanswered questions.

- Primary sources are preferred where available.
- News reports are summarized briefly and linked to the original publisher.
- Candidate responses are presented without endorsement, ranking, or editorial scoring.
- Unknown information is labelled as unknown rather than inferred.
- Corrections should be made when better documentation becomes available.
